# ⚡ ATLANTA v1.2.0 Cyber-Core Release

## 🌟 Welcome to ATLANTA — Personal AI Assistant (Version 1.2.0)

**ATLANTA** is an intelligent, cross-platform personal AI assistant inspired by Iron Man's J.A.R.V.I.S., crafted with a cutting-edge horizontal landscape cyber-tech HUD interface, high-fidelity voice interactions, and deep integration with Google Gemini Pro.

---

### 📦 Release Asset Details
* **Binary Name:** `ATLANTA.apk`
* **Target Platform:** Android (ARM64, ARMv7, x86_64) • API Level 21+ (Android 5.0 to 14+)
* **Optimized Orientation:** Landscape (Horizontal)
* **Build Mode:** Release / Standalone APK

---

### 🚀 What's New in v1.2.0

#### 1. 🌌 Frosted Glassmorphism & Cyber Glow UI
* **Frosted Glass Cards:** Dynamic `BackdropFilter` with 10px Gaussian blur and subtle translucent gradients across all dashboard and management modules.
* **Electric Neon Glows:** Configurable ambient cyan box-shadows on active IoT switches, protocol runners, and priority indicators.
* **Responsive Layouts:** Zero overflow on landscape mobile viewports (~780px to 844px width, ~360px height). Both left controls and right feeds resize smoothly.

#### 2. 🧠 Cloud Gemini AI Brain Integration
* Seamless integration with Google Gemini Pro / Flash 2.5 API for deep conversational reasoning.
* Proactive intent extraction for creating tasks, taking neural notes, scheduling reminders, controlling devices, and opening native applications.

#### 3. 🎙️ Natural Voice & Futuristic Audio Suite
* **Cinematic 6-Second Boot Sound:** Full-length opening sequence on the splash screen.
* **Greeting Voice Sequence:** Atlanta warmly welcomes the user (*"Hello Boss! Its Atlanta at your service"*) with female neural voice synthesis upon entering the dashboard.
* **Cybernetic Sound Effects:** Click SFX, holographic scanning pulses, and success chimes.
* **Live Speech Dictation:** Speech-to-text with animated audio waveforms and pulsing 3D HUD orb.

#### 4. 📱 Core Productivity & IoT Grid
* **Tasks Manager:** Priority flags, categorized tabs (Today, Upcoming, Completed), and search.
* **Neural Notes:** Color tags, pinned notes, and quick thought capture.
* **Reminders & Alarms:** Timed reminders with quick 15-minute snooze.
* **Connected Devices:** Real-time hardware mesh status (Windows PC node, Android mobile node) and IoT controls (lights, thermostats, mainframe power) with dimming and power sliders.
* **Automations Engine:** Trigger-action background workflows with live step-by-step telemetry.
* **Native App Launcher:** Direct system integration to launch Clock, Camera, Settings, YouTube, and installed apps.

---

### 📲 Installation Instructions
1. Download `ATLANTA.apk`.
2. Transfer or open the APK on your Android device.
3. If prompted, allow installation from unknown sources.
4. Rotate your device to **landscape mode** for the best holographic assistant experience.
5. Grant Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions when prompted to enable voice commands.
